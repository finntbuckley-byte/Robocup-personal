Hardware: 
Camera mount 
During the competition it would be good to capture video from the point of view of the robot. 
Provided is a camera mount that can be placed on the robot, to allow quick connection and 
disconnection of the camera. There will be only 4 cameras available. Two for those robots currently in
the arena, and 2 for those preparing to enter the arena. I know some of you have already designed 
your robots, so this is an optional feature that can be added to your if there is room. 
Battery mount 
The competition day battery will be slightly different height to the batteries currently in circulation. The 
provided battery holder allows for different sized batteries. The design of the holder allows for both 
vertical or horizontal mounting. 
General Notes: 
The plastic dummy weights should be stored at the front of the main lab and not in your storage box to
allow an equal opportunity for all teams to access the plastic weights as there is a very limited number 
of these weights. 
IMPORTANT NOTE 
Batteries need to be stored in the designed battery storage area, and not in the robot storage 
cupboard. Currently about 20% of the class are forgetting to return the battery to the storage area. 
Team or Robot Name 
Your team should think of a team name or robot name. This will be used on the competition day. An 
online form will be made available in term 4 to allow you to enter a name.
